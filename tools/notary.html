Advancing Mathematical Rigor Across Trading Card Games

This match notary utility is developed and provided completely free by Haunted Labs to advance global adoption of RFC 0001 (YGN / YBN). By transforming lossy DOM logs into clean, human-readable algebraic notation, competitive players and researchers can index matches, analyze interaction deficits, and contribute verified archetypal playlines to github.com/niloedson/ygo-archetypes.
