Haunted Labs

Founded and led by Nilo Edson, Haunted Labs is an independent software architecture studio focused on formal domain-specific language (DSL) design, deterministic combinatorial simulation engines, and autonomous Agentic AI tooling. The studio replaces lossy clickstreams and opaque binary archives with verifiable algebraic representations and high-performance analytical models.
