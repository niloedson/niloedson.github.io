Match #03: Emanuel Araújo · The Modular Substrate Mirror & MP2 Control Pivot

📊 Deck Architect Macro Analysis · Modular Mirror Substrate
Emanuel Araújo (Elfnote Hybrid · Manaus / Copa Nacional MYP): Integrates the synergistic Elfnote engine (Lucina, Regina, Fortuna, June Pride) with the shared Medius + Power Patron substrate, reinforced by high-density hand interaction (Ghost Ogre & Snow Rabbit, Droll & Lock Bird, Infinite Impermanence). This achieves a 90.4% opening consistency to establish multi-layered boards with active off-turn interaction.

TheGreatRoyal (Ars Magna & Dark Chaos Variant): Relies on the same Medius the Pure and Power Patron foundation (Jupredo, Nervedo, Vidolium, Junoldo), but branches heavily into Ars Magna and Dark Chaos ritual recursion. This creates an asymmetric mirror where interaction timing and tempo transitions determine the series.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Ghost Ogre on Continuous Spells
PSCT Clause: "When a monster on the field activates its effect, or when a Spell/Trap that is already face-up on the field activates its effect (Quick Effect): You can send this card from your hand or field to the GY; destroy that card."
Adjudication: Continuous Spells, Field Spells, and Equip Spells must remain face-up on the field to successfully resolve their on-field activated effects. By destroying Citrinitas before it resolved, Ghost Ogre caused the search effect to fizzle without resolving.
⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · 6-Link LIFO Stack Resolution
Resolving in strict reverse LIFO order, CL6 Elfnotes: Rhapsodia of Madness sent Ars Magna the Finite and the Infinite from field to GY, stripping TheGreatRoyal's defensive foundation. With his board compromised, TheGreatRoyal conceded Game 1.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Droll & Lock Bird Activation Timing
PSCT Clause: "If a card(s) is added from the Main Deck to your opponent's hand, except during the Draw Phase (Quick Effect): You can send this card from your hand to the GY; for the rest of this turn, neither player can add cards from their Main Deck to their hand."
Adjudication: Droll activates in response to a search resolving. It does not negate the initial search, but establishes a lingering game rule modification preventing any further deck-to-hand additions for both duelists.

🎙️ ygo-pilot Tactical Evaluation · Joshua Schmidt's MP2 Control Pivot
World Champion Joshua Schmidt's fundamental axiom: "If pushing for an immediate OTK requires over-committing into unverified interaction, abandon the OTK and use Main Phase 2 to construct an unbreakable control board."
Emanuel executed this heuristic flawlessly: having dealt 5,000 battle damage, he transitioned into Main Phase 2, overlaying/linking Gallant Bandit and Skull Archfiend into S:P Little Knight and setting two defensive backrow (Mind Shuffle and Typhoon).

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Dual Negation Lockout (Dragite & Photon Lord)
PSCT Clause & Resolution: Adamancipator Risen - Dragite provides a Quick Effect Spell/Trap negation while a WATER monster is in the GY, negating the GY activation of Light and Darkness Ritual. Concurrently, Number 90: Galaxy-Eyes Photon Lord detaches an Xyz Material to negate monster effect activations. Together, this multi-layered wall prevented TheGreatRoyal from resolving any GY recovery lines.

With all lines of play completely suppressed, TheGreatRoyal conceded, crowning Emanuel Araújo the series victor 2–1!
