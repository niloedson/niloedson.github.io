Match #03: The Modular Substrate Mirror & MP2 Control Pivot

Competitive Dueling Book Match analysis featuring modular engine branching, continuous spell fizzling, and World Champion Joshua Schmidt's Main Phase 2 Control Pivot.

Player 1 · Winner TheGreatRoyal Power Patron / Ars Magna / Medius / Dark Chaos
2 – 1
TheGreatRoyal Won
Player 2 Manusuperheavy Elfnote / Power Patron / Gallant Bandit
📊 Deck Architect Macro Analysis · Modular Mirror Substrate
Shared Engine Core: Both duelists run the shared Medius the Pure and Power Patron substrate (Jupredo, Nervedo, Vidolium, Junoldo).

Variant Polarization: TheGreatRoyal branches into Ars Magna and Dark Chaos ritual recursion, whereas Manusuperheavy integrates the Elfnote engine (Lucina, Regina, Fortuna, June Pride) and high-density hand traps (Ghost Ogre, Droll & Lock Bird, Ash Blossom). This creates an asymmetric matchup where play sequencing and interaction navigation decide each game.
Game 1: Impermanence Navigation & 6-Link LIFO Climax
TheGreatRoyal Won (1 - 0)

Game 1 saw TheGreatRoyal take the opening turn and immediately Normal Summon Medius the Pure. Manusuperheavy answered on summon with Infinite Impermanence, negating Medius's on-field search effect.

🎙️ ygo-pilot Tactical Evaluation · Navigating Turn 1 Interruption
A common pilot blunder is conceding tempo and passing when the normal summon is negated. TheGreatRoyal demonstrated textbook resilience: recognizing that Ars Magna the Finite and the Infinite in hand functions as an independent extender, TheGreatRoyal activated it from hand to search Division and the Union, maintaining forward combo momentum.

TheGreatRoyal linked into The Executor of the Underworld - Pluto and climbed into Purification Power Patron, eventually establishing Number 90: Galaxy-Eyes Photon Lord and Elfnote Seraphim Strelitzia.

On Turn 2, Manusuperheavy attempted to disrupt TheGreatRoyal's backrow by chaining Ghost Ogre & Snow Rabbit to Ars Magna "Citrinitas".

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Ghost Ogre on Continuous Spells
PSCT Clause: "When a monster on the field activates its effect, or when a Spell/Trap that is already face-up on the field activates its effect (Quick Effect): You can send this card from your hand or field to the GY; destroy that card."
Adjudication: Continuous Spells, Field Spells, and Equip Spells must remain face-up on the field to successfully resolve their on-field activated effects. By destroying Citrinitas before it resolved, Ghost Ogre caused the search effect to fizzle without resolving.

The turn reached a dramatic climax when a massive 6-link chain unfolded: CL1 Black Chaos → CL2 Photon Lord → CL3 Junoldo → CL4 Past Lull → CL5 Past Lull → CL6 Elfnotes: Rhapsodia of Madness.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · 6-Link LIFO Stack Resolution
Resolving in strict reverse LIFO order, CL6 Rhapsodia provided defense, but CL2 Photon Lord successfully negated and destroyed the key opposing monster asset. With all follow-up resources neutralized, Manusuperheavy conceded Game 1.

Game 2: Ghost Ogre Baits & The Elfnote Surge
TheGreatRoyal Won (2 - 0)

In Game 2, TheGreatRoyal baited out Manusuperheavy's Ghost Ogre & Snow Rabbit early by committing Artmage Non-Finito to the field before activating Artmage Vandalism -Assault-.

Manusuperheavy followed up with Droll & Lock Bird after Medius was added to hand, preventing further deck searches for the remainder of the turn.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Droll & Lock Bird Activation Timing
PSCT Clause: "If a card(s) is added from the Main Deck to your opponent's hand, except during the Draw Phase (Quick Effect): You can send this card from your hand to the GY; for the rest of this turn, neither player can add cards from their Main Deck to their hand."
Adjudication: Droll activates in response to a search resolving. It does not negate the initial search, but places a lingering floodgate preventing any further deck-to-hand additions for either duelist.

On Turn 2, Manusuperheavy swarmed with Elfnote Lucina, Regina, and June Pride, pushing 2,500 combat damage. However, on Turn 3, TheGreatRoyal orchestrated a ritual turnaround with Magician of Dark Chaos - Black Chaos, linking into Mediclius the Extraordinary Power Patron to sweep the board and claim Game 2.

Game 3: 5000 Battle Tempo & The MP2 Control Pivot
TheGreatRoyal Won (2 - 1)

Game 3 began with high intensity: Manusuperheavy dropped Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring on TheGreatRoyal's Prohibited Power Patron Portal on Turn 1.

Seizing the initiative on Turn 2, Manusuperheavy Normal Summoned The Great Gallant Bandit and revived Skull Archfiend of Chaos from the graveyard. Declaring direct attacks with both 2,500 ATK monsters, Manusuperheavy inflicted 5,000 direct combat damage in a single battle phase!

🎙️ ygo-pilot Tactical Evaluation · Joshua Schmidt's MP2 Control Pivot
World Champion Joshua Schmidt's fundamental axiom: "If going for game requires over-committing into unknown backrow or risking an unverified out, abandon the OTK immediately and use Main Phase 2 to establish control."
Manusuperheavy executed this heuristic flawlessly: after dealing 5,000 battle damage, Manusuperheavy transitioned into Main Phase 2, overlaying/linking Gallant Bandit and Skull Archfiend into S:P Little Knight and setting two defensive backrow (Mind Shuffle and Typhoon).

Facing lethal on Turn 4, TheGreatRoyal responded with ruthless precision on Turn 3: activating Ars Magna the Corruption and the Purification to banish Manusuperheavy's set Typhoon, linking into S:P Little Knight to banish Mind Shuffle, and Special Summoning Number 90: Galaxy-Eyes Photon Lord to dismantle the remaining defenses and close out the match 2–1.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Backrow Banishment Before Activation
Interaction: Corruption and the Purification targeted and banished the face-down Typhoon. Because Typhoon was face-down and lacked a valid quick-effect trigger condition at that moment, it could not be chained in response, cleanly depriving Manusuperheavy of interaction.
