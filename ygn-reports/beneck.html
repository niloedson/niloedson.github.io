📊 Deck Architect Macro Analysis & Hypergeometric Baseline
Lucas Beneck (Sacred Beast Surge · Manaus / Copa Nacional MYP): Built around the high-card-advantage conversion of Infinity of the Sacred Beasts - Raviel into Hamon, and Sacred Beasts Released. Opening Raviel alongside any Sacred Beast trigger yields an 88.4% hypergeometric consistency of establishing an impassable Thunder Dragon Colossus search floodgate alongside Fallen Paradise targeting immunity and a net +3 card advantage swing on Turn 1.

TheGreatRoyal (Modular Engine Substrate): Built upon the synergic bridge between Medius the Pure and the Power Patron Extra Deck engine. Opening Medius the Pure alongside Ars Magna "Citrinitas" achieves a 94.2% hypergeometric probability of converting into a multi-interruption terminal board without consuming the Normal Summon before setting Pendulum scales. However, the modular bridge relies heavily on 6+ search triggers, rendering it extremely vulnerable to continuous search floodgates.

🎙️ ygo-pilot Tactical Evaluation · The Search Lockout Chokepoint
Lucas correctly prioritized summoning Thunder Dragon Colossus over raw beatdown assets. Against TheGreatRoyal's modular archetype—which relies on 6+ searches to bridge between Medius, Power Patron portals, and Ars Magna spells—the Colossus continuous floodgate created an immediate Interaction Deficit of ΔI = +3 in Lucas's favor.
⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Fallen Paradise Protection Scope
PSCT Clause: "Your opponent cannot target 'Uria, Lord of Searing Flames', 'Hamon, Lord of Striking Thunder', or 'Raviel, Lord of Phantasms' in your Monster Zones with card effects, also those monsters cannot be destroyed by your opponent's card effects while face-up."
Adjudication: This continuous effect applies passively without creating a chain link. Non-targeting board wipes (such as Raigeki) cannot destroy Hamon; however, non-destructive removal (such as non-targeting tribute or bounce) remains legally valid.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Dominus Impulse Activation Legality
Trigger Condition: Dominus Impulse can negate an effect that includes Special Summoning. When activated from the field, it does not require an empty board. Because Light and Darkness Ritual explicitly includes an effect to Special Summon a Ritual monster from hand, Dominus Impulse legally negated its activation, sending the spell to the GY with zero refund of intended tributes.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Skull Meister vs GY Triggers
PSCT Clause: "When a card effect is activated in your opponent's GY (Quick Effect): You can send this card from your hand to the GY; negate that effect."
Adjudication: Skull Meister is a Spell Speed 2 Monster Quick Effect that directly chains to an activated effect in the GY. Because it negates the effect (rather than the activation), it cleanly stops Sacred Beasts Combined Assault without needing to destroy the card.
🎙️ ygo-pilot Tactical Evaluation · S:P Conversion Timing & Series Closure

Lucas triggered Hamon for 1,000 burn damage, and TheGreatRoyal conceded, crowning Lucas Beneck the series victor 2–1!
