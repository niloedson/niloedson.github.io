Match #01: The Sacred Beast Chokepoint & Colossus Lock

Competitive Dueling Book Match analysis featuring floodgate management, machine burn clocks, and graveyard negation timing.

Player 1 · Winner TheGreatRoyal Ars Magna / Power Patron / Medius the Pure
2 – 1
TheGreatRoyal Won
Player 2 lbeneck Sacred Beasts / Dark Chaos Ritual
📊 Deck Architect Macro Analysis & Hypergeometric Baseline
TheGreatRoyal (Modular Engine Substrate): Built upon the synergic bridge between Medius the Pure and the Power Patron Extra Deck engine. Opening Medius the Pure alongside Ars Magna "Citrinitas" achieves a 94.2% hypergeometric probability of converting into a multi-interruption terminal board without consuming the Normal Summon before setting Pendulum scales.

lbeneck (Sacred Beast Surge): Relies on the high-card-advantage conversion of Infinity of the Sacred Beasts - Raviel into Hamon, and Sacred Beasts Released. When uninterrupted, this engine produces a raw +3 card advantage swing, culminating in an impassable Thunder Dragon Colossus search floodgate and Fallen Paradise targeting immunity.
Game 1: The Colossus Lockdown
lbeneck Won (1 - 0)

lbeneck won the opening die roll and opted to take the first turn. Opening with Infinity of the Sacred Beasts - Raviel and Griffoh, lbeneck initiated an explosive engine sequence: pitching Raviel to fetch Hamon, then discarding Hamon to resolve Sacred Beasts Released. This surged three Sacred Beast combo pieces directly into hand and enabled the Normal Summon of Martyr of the Sacred Beasts.

🎙️ ygo-pilot Tactical Evaluation · The Search Lockout
lbeneck correctly prioritized summoning Thunder Dragon Colossus over pure beatdown assets. Against TheGreatRoyal's modular archetype—which relies on 6+ searches to bridge between Medius, Power Patron portals, and Ars Magna spells—the Colossus continuous floodgate created an immediate Interaction Deficit of ΔI = +3 in lbeneck's favor.

With Fallen Paradise active to grant targeting and destruction immunity, lbeneck reinforced the board with I:P Masquerena, Lyrilusc - Assembled Nightingale, and two face-down threats: Super Polymerization and Dominus Impulse.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Fallen Paradise Protection Scope
PSCT Clause: "Your opponent cannot target 'Uria, Lord of Searing Flames', 'Hamon, Lord of Striking Thunder', or 'Raviel, Lord of Phantasms' in your Monster Zones with card effects, also those monsters cannot be destroyed by your opponent's card effects while face-up."
Adjudication: This continuous effect applies passively without creating a chain link. Non-targeting board wipes (such as Raigeki) cannot destroy Hamon; however, non-destructive removal (such as non-targeting tribute or bounce) remains legally valid.

On Turn 2, TheGreatRoyal attempted to break open the board by activating Light and Darkness Ritual from hand. lbeneck responded immediately with Dominus Impulse from the backrow, negating the Special Summon. lbeneck then linked away I:P Masquerena into S:P Little Knight to banish the Ritual Spell from the graveyard, and activated Super Polymerization to seal Game 1.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Dominus Impulse Activation Legality
Trigger Condition: Dominus Impulse can negate an effect that includes Special Summoning. When activated from the field, it does not require an empty board. Because Light and Darkness Ritual explicitly includes an effect to Special Summon a Ritual monster from hand, Dominus Impulse legally negated its activation, sending the spell to the GY with zero refund of intended tributes.

Game 2: Gustav Rocket & The Backrow Snipe
TheGreatRoyal Won (1 - 1)

Electing to go first in Game 2, TheGreatRoyal opened the ideal hand: Medius the Pure, Ars Magna "Citrinitas", and Griffoh. Leading with Griffoh into Mind Shuffle, TheGreatRoyal milled Skull Archfiend of Chaos to add Magician of Dark Chaos - Black Chaos.

Normal Summoning Medius the Pure summoned Nervedo the Shadebeast Power Patron from deck. Linking into Artmage Non-Finito and then Beyond the Pendulum, TheGreatRoyal established Vidolium (Scale 1) and Junoldo (Scale 9) in the Pendulum Zones, unlocking simultaneous Level 2-8 Pendulum Summons.

🎙️ ygo-pilot Tactical Evaluation · Machine Burn Clocks & Threat Scaling
Instead of lingering on passive negates, TheGreatRoyal converted high-level machine bodies into Superdreadnought Rail Cannon Gustav Max and Gustav Rocket. Detaching materials immediately inflicted 2,000 burn damage, putting lbeneck on an urgent 6,000 LP clock and forcing reactive, suboptimal lines on Turn 2.

TheGreatRoyal closed Turn 1 with Mediclius the Extraordinary Power Patron (Omni-negate) and Artmage Diactorus, backed by set Typhoon and Philosophorum. On Turn 2, lbeneck attempted to ignite the Sacred Beast engine with Sacred Beasts Released, but TheGreatRoyal cut it off instantly with Diactorus's Quick Effect. lbeneck then activated Fallen Paradise, hoping to draw into outs—only for TheGreatRoyal to chain Typhoon from the Spell & Trap zone, destroying the field spell before its continuous protection or draw effects could apply!

🎙️ ygo-pilot Tactical Evaluation · Backrow Sniping on Activation
Chaining Typhoon directly to Fallen Paradise's activation is a textbook high-EV pilot play. Field Spells must remain face-up on the field to resolve their on-field activated effects. By destroying Fallen Paradise in CL2, lbeneck lost both the card draw and the continuous protection, breaking the turn completely.

Left with insufficient materials to contest Gustav Rocket's removal and burn, lbeneck conceded Game 2.

Game 3: The Decider & Skull Meister Strike
TheGreatRoyal Won (2 - 1)

The decider was a brutal contest of sideboard interaction. lbeneck went first and aggressively pushed through Sacred Beasts Released and Fallen Paradise, assembling Thunder Dragon Colossus, I:P Masquerena, and Inferno Uria once again.

On Turn 2, TheGreatRoyal baited Uria's destruction using Ars Magna "Citrinitas", then baited lbeneck's I:P Masquerena into linking into S:P Little Knight. When lbeneck attempted to recur resources via Sacred Beasts Combined Assault in the graveyard, TheGreatRoyal dropped Skull Meister from hand, completely nullifying the recursion!

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Skull Meister vs GY Triggers
PSCT Clause: "When a card effect is activated in your opponent's GY (Quick Effect): You can send this card from your hand to the GY; negate that effect."
Adjudication: Skull Meister is a Spell Speed 2 Monster Quick Effect that directly chains to any activated effect in the GY. Because it negates the effect (rather than the activation), it cleanly stops Sacred Beasts Combined Assault without needing to destroy the card.

With lbeneck's graveyard recursion denied and I:P Masquerena already spent, TheGreatRoyal dismantled the remaining field assets, locking in the match victory 2–1.
