📊 Deck Architect Macro Analysis · Engine Synergies
Jacson Moura (Radiant Typhoon Engine · Manaus / Copa Nacional MYP): A high-synergy WIND Beast-Warrior swarm strategy searching key starters via Fire Formation - Tenki with a 91.8% opening consistency to establish Swen and Eldam. The deck's primary win condition revolves around establishing Totem Bird to lock down opposing spells, supported by devastating board-breakers (Super Polymerization) and disruptive silver bullets (Artifact Lancea).

TheGreatRoyal (Modular Engine Substrate): Combines ritual recursion via Ragged Records of Rites with Medius the Pure's machine engine. This allows assembling multi-negate boards (Number 38 for Spell negation and Mediclius for monster/omni negation) while maintaining follow-up resources in the Extra Deck.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Velocidade de Magia 4 (Spell Speed 4) vs Number 38
PSCT Clause: "Neither player can activate cards or effects in response to this card's activation."
Adjudication: Despite Number 38: Hope Harbinger having an active Quick Effect that negates Spell cards, it cannot legally chain to Super Polymerization. Operating at Velocidade de Magia 4 (Spell Speed 4), the fusion resolved unhindered, stripping TheGreatRoyal of two key assets with zero response window.
⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · 5-Link Chain (Chain Link 5) LIFO Stack Resolution
Chain Stack Construction:
• CL1: Krosea activates in hand to Special Summon itself.
• CL2: Ash Blossom responds from opponent's hand.
• CL3: Mediclius activates to negate Mudragon.
• CL4: Radiant Typhoon Vision responds.
• CL5: Number 38: Hope Harbinger activates, detaching material to negate Vision.

Resolution in Reverse LIFO Order (Chain Link 5):
• R5: Number 38 negates and attaches Vision.
• R4: Vision is negated.
• R3: Mediclius negates Mudragon.
• R2: Ash Blossom resolves.
• R1: Krosea resolves its summon without further deck searching.

With his remaining extenders suppressed by TheGreatRoyal's residual negations, Jacson conceded Game 1.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Artifact Lancea Lingering Banish Lock
PSCT Clause: "During your opponent's turn (Quick Effect): You can tribute this card from your hand or face-up field; neither player can banish cards for the rest of this turn."
Adjudication: Lancea applies a lingering game rule modification. Cards that require banishing as an activation cost (such as Ars Magna fusion spells, Medius GY triggers, or Griffoh) cannot legally be activated because a player cannot attempt an action that requires an illegal payment.

Struggling under the Lancea banish lock, TheGreatRoyal attempted to activate Pendulum Treasure to setup scales manually. Jacson detached from Totem Bird to negate the activation and destroy the card. Paralyzed without banishing or spells, TheGreatRoyal conceded Game 2.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Predaplant Dragostapelia Predator Counters
PSCT Clause: "Once per turn (Quick Effect): You can target 1 face-up monster your opponent controls; place 1 Predator Counter on it... Negate the activated effects of any opponent's monster that has a Predator Counter."
Adjudication: Dragostapelia targeted Bandit, placing a Predator Counter to negate its activated effects. However, Dragostapelia's negation does not prevent Bandit from participating in combat or being used as Link material.
🎙️ ygo-pilot Tactical Evaluation · Fonix Double-Spin & Wynn Lethal Push
Jacson executed a masterstroke tactical sequence: Special Summoning Radiant Typhoon Fonix, the Great Flame to spin both Dragostapelia and Mediclius back to the Extra Deck with non-destructive removal. Jacson then linked into Wynn the Wind Charmer, Verdant, reviving The Great Gallant Bandit from the graveyard and pushing through over 7,600 direct battle damage to close Game 3 and clinch the series 2–1!
