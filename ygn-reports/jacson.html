Match #02: Clash of the Typhoons & 5-Link Chain Resolution

Competitive Dueling Book Match analysis featuring unchainable fusion board-breakers, deep reverse LIFO chain unwinding, and lingering banish floodgates.

Player 1 · Winner TheGreatRoyal Medius / Power Patron / Dark Chaos / Artmage
2 – 1
TheGreatRoyal Won
Player 2 jacsonmoura Radiant Typhoon (Wind Beast-Warrior)
📊 Deck Architect Macro Analysis · Engine Synergies
TheGreatRoyal: Combines ritual recursion via Ragged Records of Rites with Medius the Pure's machine engine. This allows assembling multi-negate boards (Number 38 for Spell negation and Mediclius for monster/omni negation) while maintaining follow-up resources in the Extra Deck.

jacsonmoura (Radiant Typhoon Engine): A WIND Beast-Warrior swarm strategy searching key starters via Fire Formation - Tenki. The deck's primary win condition is establishing Totem Bird to lock down opposing spells, supported by powerful board-breakers (Super Polymerization) and disruptive silver bullets (Artifact Lancea).
Game 1: The Layered Citadel & The 5-Link Chain
TheGreatRoyal Won (1 - 0)

TheGreatRoyal took the first turn and opened with Medius the Pure and Ragged Records of Rites. Using Ragged Records to pitch Griffoh and retrieve Light and Darkness Ritual, TheGreatRoyal summoned Magician of Dark Chaos to recycle resources before Normal Summoning Medius.

The combo proceeded through Beyond the Pendulum into Gustav Max, Gustav Rocket, and Mediclius the Extraordinary Power Patron, concluding with the Xyz Summon of Number 38: Hope Harbinger Dragon Titanic Galaxy.

On Turn 2, jacsonmoura attempted to break the formidable board immediately by dropping Super Polymerization, fusing TheGreatRoyal's Artmage Non-Finito and Gustav Rocket into Mudragon of the Swamp.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Super Polymerization vs Number 38
PSCT Clause: "Neither player can activate cards or effects in response to this card's activation."
Adjudication: Despite Number 38: Hope Harbinger having an active Quick Effect that negates Spell cards, it cannot legally chain to Super Polymerization. The fusion resolved unhindered, stripping TheGreatRoyal of two key monsters without allowing spell negation response.

Following the fusion summon, an extraordinary 5-link chain erupted when jacsonmoura activated Radiant Typhoon Krosea in hand:

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · 5-Link LIFO Chain Stack Resolution
Chain Building:
• CL1: Krosea activates in hand to Special Summon itself.
• CL2: Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring responds from hand.
• CL3: Mediclius the Extraordinary Power Patron activates to negate Mudragon.
• CL4: Radiant Typhoon Vision responds.
• CL5: Number 38: Hope Harbinger activates, detaching material to negate Vision.

Chain Resolution (Reverse LIFO Order):
• R5: Number 38 successfully negates and absorbs Vision.
• R4: Vision is negated (fizzles).
• R3: Mediclius negates Mudragon.
• R2: Ash Blossom resolves.
• R1: Krosea resolves its summon without further deck searching.

With all major extenders suppressed by TheGreatRoyal's layered negations, jacsonmoura conceded Game 1.

Game 2: Artifact Lancea & Totem Bird Lock
jacsonmoura Won (1 - 1)

Going first in Game 2, jacsonmoura showcased the full consistency of the Radiant Typhoon engine. Leading with Fire Formation - Tenki to search Swen, jacsonmoura swarmed the field with Eldam, Varuroon, and Meghala.

🎙️ ygo-pilot Tactical Evaluation · Proactive Totem Bird Setup
jacsonmoura recognized that TheGreatRoyal's engine relies heavily on normal and continuous Spells (Theorealize, Ars Magna, Pendulum Treasure). Overlaying two Level 3 WIND monsters into Totem Bird created an on-field Spell/Trap omni-negate that directly checked TheGreatRoyal's ignition lines.

On Turn 2, jacsonmoura immediately dropped Artifact Lancea from hand during the Draw Phase.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Artifact Lancea Lingering Banish Lock
PSCT Clause: "During your opponent's turn (Quick Effect): You can tribute this card from your hand or face-up field; neither player can banish cards for the rest of this turn."
Adjudication: Lancea applies a lingering game rule modification. Cards that require banishing as an activation cost (such as Ars Magna fusion spells, Medius GY triggers, or Griffoh) cannot legally be activated because a player cannot attempt an action that requires an illegal payment.

Struggling under the Lancea banish lock, TheGreatRoyal attempted to activate Pendulum Treasure to setup scales manually. jacsonmoura detached from Totem Bird to negate the activation and destroy the card. Paralyzed without banishing or spells, TheGreatRoyal conceded Game 2.

Game 3: The Decider & Dragostapelia Predator Counters
TheGreatRoyal Won (2 - 1)

Game 3 saw jacsonmoura shotgunning Artifact Lancea during the Draw Phase once again. However, TheGreatRoyal adapted, playing through the graveyard via Skull Archfiend of Chaos and Light and Darkness Ritual without relying on early banishing.

On Turn 2, jacsonmoura Normal Summoned The Great Gallant Bandit and attempted to build momentum with Noble Knight's Shield-Bearer and Radiant Typhoon Eldam. TheGreatRoyal responded by Fusion Summoning Predaplant Dragostapelia.

⚖️ ygo-judge Ruling Review · Predaplant Dragostapelia Predator Counters
PSCT Clause: "Once per turn (Quick Effect): You can target 1 face-up monster your opponent controls; place 1 Predator Counter on it... Negate the activated effects of any opponent's monster that has a Predator Counter."
Adjudication: Dragostapelia targeted The Great Gallant Bandit, placing a Predator Counter on it. This negated Bandit's activated effects continuously while on field. In the same chain, TheGreatRoyal's Ash Blossom intercepted Eldam's deck search, shutting down jacsonmoura's extenders simultaneously.

jacsonmoura attempted to pivot into Wynn the Wind Charmer, Verdant to revive Gallant Bandit for an attack, but TheGreatRoyal cleanly dismantled the remaining board during the battle phase, locking up the series 2–1.
